# india.gov.in schemes compared with SchemeSetu (2026-11-21)

## Summary
- india.gov.in's scheme section (https://www.india.gov.in/my-government/schemes) is **powered by myScheme**: it shows the
  myScheme logo, and every scheme links to `https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/<slug>`, the same links SchemeSetu stores.
  It is not a separate source with its own schemes.
- It says 5,791 schemes, but that count includes 765 duplicates: **5,026 different schemes**.
- **115** of them are not in SchemeSetu. **252** of SchemeSetu's schemes are not on india.gov.in (its copy of myScheme
  is older than ours). No scheme has the same title under a different link.
- The 115 still have full pages on myScheme, but **myScheme's own search no longer lists them** (myScheme shows
  5,194 schemes; searching for one of them by its exact name finds nothing). That is why the weekly job never picked them up.
- Many look like closed or one-off programmes (SERB awards: SERB was replaced by ANRF in 2024; yearly national awards),
  but not all (e.g. a Rajasthan PM-KUSUM component). myScheme does not say why they were taken out of the list.

**Not added.** Adding them would need the owner's decision (see the options in the chat of 2026-11-21).

## Categories of the 115
Social welfare 49, Agriculture 34, Education 23, Skills 8, Women and child 6, Business 5, Health 5, Banking 4,
Science/IT 3, Housing 3, Sports 2, Travel 1 (a scheme can be in more than one).
